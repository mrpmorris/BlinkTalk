Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

BlinkTalk is a free augmentative and alternative communication app published by Air Software Limited.

1. No data collection

BlinkTalk does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or store personal data on our servers. It has no accounts, adverts, analytics, tracking, cookies, advertising identifiers, or third-party advertising SDKs.

2. Camera use

BlinkTalk may use your device camera to recognise eye or face gestures selected by you. Camera processing happens entirely on your device. BlinkTalk does not record, save, upload, or share video or images.

3. Local app data

Text entered into BlinkTalk, sentence history, settings, and learned word predictions are stored only on your device. This information is never uploaded or shared.

4. Other data

BlinkTalk does not collect location, contacts, microphone audio, health information, payment information, or device identifiers.

5. Deleting data

You can remove all BlinkTalk data from your device by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

6. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The updated version will appear on this page with a new date.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact mrpmorris@gmail.com.
