BlinkTalk

Free communication software that helps people with locked-in syndrome and other motor conditions talk again — using just a single switch, a tap, or even a blink.

Get BlinkTalk

How it works

1

Scan the options

The screen highlights one choice at a time on a timer. Everything on screen takes turns lighting up.

2

Make a single signal

A helper taps the screen, presses Space, or the camera detects a blink. That one signal selects whatever is highlighted.

3

Build words and speak

Pick suggested words or spell out letters. BlinkTalk predicts what you are likely to say next and speaks the sentence aloud.
